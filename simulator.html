HOME SCREEN

1
Calorie ring shows today's progress toward that athlete's specific goal
2
Weight tracker card shows gain from start weight toward target
3
Profile avatar (initials) in top-right — tap to switch athletes

SWITCHING ATHLETES

1
Tap the initials avatar in the top-right corner
→
A sheet slides up showing all athletes
2
Tap any athlete to switch — the whole app reloads with their data, goals, and weight targets
3
Each athlete has completely separate meal logs and weigh-ins — nothing overlaps

ADDING A NEW ATHLETE

1
Parent taps the avatar → profile sheet → "+ Add Athlete"
2
Fill in name, sport, calorie goal, start weight, target weight, PIN
3
Profile is created. App automatically switches to the new athlete.
4
Then go to Parent Dashboard → Share Setup Link to send it to that athlete's phone

PARENT DASHBOARD

1
Tap "PARENT" in the top-right — enter your 4-digit PIN
2
See the active athlete's full day: calories, meals, protein, last ate
3
"Share Daily Summary" copies a text report to clipboard — paste into iMessage
4
"Share Setup Link" generates a QR + URL to send to the athlete's phone

SHARING THE SETUP LINK

1
Parent sets up the athlete's profile first (see "Add Athlete" flow)
2
Switch to that athlete → open Parent Dashboard → tap "Share Setup Link"
3
QR code appears — athlete scans it, or parent copies the link and texts it
4
The link contains the athlete's full config — no typing required on their end

ATHLETE IMPORTS THEIR PROFILE

1
Athlete opens the link (from text or QR scan)
2
They see a confirmation screen with their name, sport, and goals — no typing
3
One tap: "Set Up My App" — profile is created, app is ready to use
4
Their data stays on their device. Parents can't see it unless they have the PIN.
